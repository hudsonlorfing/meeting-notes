Hudson conducted a discovery call with Tarun, founder of Calibrate, a cold email outreach service. Calibrate offers end-to-end cold email campaigns at industrial scale using multiple domains and AI-powered personalization. They charge $5,250/month for 10,000 outreach volume and promise 60-day setup with results typically appearing in month 3. # Key Points

## Chrt's Current Situation
- **Product Evolution**: Originally focused only on couriers ($100-2,500/month), now expanding to freight forwarders and shippers due to market demand
- **Revenue Acceleration**: Three current customers could generate $5-15M MRR, forcing faster development timeline
- **Gating Strategy**: Currently using waitlist to control customer onboarding pace while building infrastructure
- **Team Structure**: Sales team is just Kyle (co-founder) and Hudson; planning to add headcount in Q2

> "We have three customers that them alone could bring on anywhere from 5 to 15 million in MRR, which is petrifying to me because I got. And it's like, yeah, cool. And then you look at all the work behind it and you're like, that's fast." - Hudson

## Target Customer Segmentation
- **Shippers**: Healthcare companies (Stryker, MedTech), anyone needing to ship time-critical items
- **Freight Forwarders**: Coordinate shipments between shippers and couriers
- **Couriers**: Most challenging segment - fleet operators, individual drivers, scattered across various industries
- **Geographic Focus**: Currently US-based operations

> "The biggest, the most scattered group of customers that we have to reach are called couriers. So it's basically anyone that owns or operates a fleet of drivers. Whether that is you are managing W2 drivers or you personally own drivers and you deploy them, that's the harder one to reach" - Hudson

## Calibrate's Service Model
- **Infrastructure**: 5-50 lookalike domains, each with 5-100 mailboxes, sending 5-20 emails/day per mailbox
- **Volume Commitment**: Base plan targets 10,000 leads/month with 1,000 emails/day
- **Timeline**: 14-20 day warmup period, results typically in month 3
- **Personalization**: AI-powered using any online data - LinkedIn, websites, Google Maps, YouTube comments, Reddit threads
- **Management**: Unified dashboard for reply management, bi-monthly strategy calls

## Technical Integration Discussion
Hudson explored API integration possibilities for automated responses:
- Chrt has sophisticated backend data classification (freight forwarder vs courier, healthcare vs general freight, regional specializations)
- Interest in templated responses based on prospect classification
- Calibrate offers API access to positive replies for custom automation
- Hudson prefers building integration in-house rather than custom development by Calibrate

## Pricing Structure
- **Base Plan**: $5,250/month for 10,000 outreach volume
- **Scaling**: Next tier ~$7,500 (not linear scaling)
- **Service Level**: End-to-end management with dedicated customer success manager
- **Timeline**: 60-day commitment recommended (setup + optimization + results)

## Lead Generation Capabilities
Calibrate can source leads from multiple channels:
- Traditional B2B databases and LinkedIn
- **Google Maps scraping**: Search "couriers in Texas" and extract contact information
- **Social media mining**: YouTube comments, Reddit threads, forum posts for community-driven industries like logistics
- **Intent data**: Track companies searching for relevant keywords (via Bombora partnership)

> "We could say, hey, on this video, check the comments or any comment from these guys. Could you figure out the comments, trace back and then figure out how to get that lead list?" - Hudson
> "Yes. If they have their email ID attached to their YouTube channel publicly, we can scrape every comment, then scrape the emails behind each of those profiles and then outreach to them." - Tarun
