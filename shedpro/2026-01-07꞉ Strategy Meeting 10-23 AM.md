## Executive Summary

Hudson conducted a comprehensive analysis of ShedPro's resource allocation using time tracking data (T-metric), revealing critical operational inefficiencies. The analysis shows that 40% of development team time is spent in meetings, 30% on internal roadmap work, leaving only 30% for revenue-generating client activities. This misallocation explains persistent capacity constraints and development bottlenecks. Key strategic decisions emerged around pricing alignment with competitors (moving from $495 to $690 monthly for 3D configurator), resource reallocation from meetings to client work, and prioritizing Project Atlas - a revolutionary modeling system that could eliminate custom model bottlenecks.

## Key Strategic Insights

### Resource Allocation Crisis
**Current Development Team Time Split:**
- **40% Meetings & Admin** (18% internal meetings + training/events)
- **30% Internal Roadmap** (not client-specific)
- **30% Client Work** (actual revenue generation)

> "So 40% of your time right now is meetings and roadmap and 20% is revenue" - Hudson

This explains Wu's constant "no capacity" responses - the team has systematically filled development capacity with internal work, leaving insufficient time for new client onboarding.

### Pricing Strategy Misalignment
**Current vs. Market Pricing:**
- ShedPro 3D Configurator: $495/month
- Idea Room (competitor): $690/month
- **Opportunity:** 93 clients within $100 of current pricing could generate +$40k MRR with price adjustment

> "Patrick last night... he was saying, so as far as I know, Idea room, their bottom tier comparable product to ours is 690amonth. So ours is 500. So Bach's going, why you guys cheaper just be the same price." - James

### Project Atlas - Game-Changing Solution
Wu has been developing Project Atlas during overtime - a system that could transform custom model creation from a multi-person, multi-week process to simple configuration settings. This addresses the core bottleneck causing development resource constraints.

## Strategic Decisions Made

### 1. Resource Reallocation Mandate
**Decision:** Immediately restructure development team allocation
- **Target:** 50% client work, 20% meetings, 30% roadmap
- **Current:** 30% client work, 40% meetings, 30% roadmap
- **Impact:** Effectively doubles client-facing capacity

### 2. Pricing Strategy Alignment
**Decision:** Move to market-competitive pricing
- 3D Configurator: $495 → $690/month
- Implement graduated increases for existing clients
- Immediate focus on 93 clients near current pricing threshold

### 3. Project Atlas Prioritization
**Decision:** Make Project Atlas the #1 development priority
- Potential to eliminate custom model bottlenecks
- Transform onboarding from weeks to days
- Enable significant setup fee reduction with higher monthly values
