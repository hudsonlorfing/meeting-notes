## Operational Lift Assessment

### 3D Configurator Setup
**Operational Requirements:** Client-specific configuration of software, model library setup, pricing integration
**Complexity Level:** Varies significantly based on standard vs custom models
**Quote:** > "We configure it to the software as well as to the client, which is why it's not applied. It's not everyone does it different."

### Model Implementation
**Operational Requirements:** 
- Standard models: Minimal setup effort, highly profitable
- Custom models: Significant development time, requires CTO approval
**Complexity Level:** Nick characterized as ranging from "no brainer" to "quite the list to get that right"
**Quote:** > "We love contracts with lots of standard models. That is way easier, way more profitable."

### Service Delivery (Ongoing)
**Operational Requirements:** Project coordinators handle settings changes, pricing updates, feature toggles
**Complexity Level:** Routine maintenance handled by service delivery team
**Quote:** > "Maintenance would be something like. Hey. Update my pricing data or hey, in the past we never showed pricing. Now we want to turn that pricing on."
