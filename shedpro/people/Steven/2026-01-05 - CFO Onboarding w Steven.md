## Executive Summary
This meeting is an onboarding session for a new CFO to ShedPro. The discussion focuses on ShedPro's business model, customer acquisition strategies, and product development challenges related to their 3D configurator for portable buildings. Key topics include the tension between custom model development and scalable solutions, the importance of the carport market, and the need for better data tracking to inform product decisions. The CFO is seeking to understand the drivers of cost and revenue to improve pricing power and resource allocation.

## Pain Points

### Pain 1: Tension between Custom Models and Scalability
- **Description:** Sales wants to offer custom models to capture market share, but development (Vu) prioritizes scalable solutions. This leads to internal conflict and potentially missed opportunities.
- **Quote:** "So that's where a lot of our tension comes from, is, you know, me wanting to sell something, him not wanting to sell it, and sometimes me feeling like, okay, we should sell this because it'll open up more market share."
- **Severity:** 🟡 Medium
- **Impact:** Limits market reach, internal friction, potential loss of revenue.

### Pain 2: Limited Carport Configurator Capabilities
- **Description:** ShedPro's configurator lacks comprehensive carport features, causing them to lose deals to competitors and limiting their ability to serve the carport market.
- **Quote:** "So because our carport solution is not good...Not good because your models are good or because it's...It's not...Because it's limited. It's very limited."
- **Severity:** 🔴 High
- **Impact:** Lost sales, inability to penetrate a large market segment (carports), hindering growth.

### Pain 3: Lack of Data-Driven Product Decisions
- **Description:** Decisions about custom model development and feature prioritization are often based on hunches rather than concrete data. There's no systematic way to substantiate the value of custom features.
- **Quote:** "It's a hunch. It's a sense. Even if I'm right, I can't substantiate, like, the value. We don't have a good way to do that right now."
- **Severity:** 🟡 Medium
- **Impact:** Inefficient resource allocation, potentially developing features that aren't widely used, missed opportunities.

### Pain 4: Inefficient Self-Service for Settings Adjustments
- **Description:** Clients cannot easily adjust settings or prices themselves, requiring ShedPro's service delivery team to handle these requests, increasing COGS.
- **Quote:** "So actually in terms of our like cogs on subscription, if we could figure out a way for people to be more self service that would be a big benefit to everybody."
- **Severity:** 🔵 Low
- **Impact:** Higher operational costs, less efficient use of service delivery team's time.
