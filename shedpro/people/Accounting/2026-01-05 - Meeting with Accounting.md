## Executive Summary
This meeting with the accounting team focuses on refining financial reporting to provide better insights into product profitability and customer segmentation. Key discussions revolve around implementing tracking categories in Xero to separate revenue and costs for setup, recurring subscriptions, and custom models. The goal is to enable data-driven decision-making for sales, pricing, and resource allocation, particularly regarding custom 3D models which are suspected to be less profitable. The team also discussed moving payment processing fees into cost of goods sold for SaaS revenue recognition, and the need to improve data integrity and time tracking accuracy for operations.

## Current State
**Tech Stack:** Xero (accounting software), Stripe (payment processing), Fathom (financial analysis and consolidation), potentially Team Metric (time tracking)
**Operations:** Current financial reporting lacks detailed categorization by product and customer type. Time tracking is manual and doesn't differentiate between setup, ongoing, and custom work.
**Vendors:** Stripe, Xero, potentially Fathom.

## Pain Points

### Pain 1: Inaccurate Profitability Measurement of Custom 3D Models
- **Description:** The current accounting system doesn't accurately track the costs associated with custom 3D models, making it difficult to determine their profitability.
- **Quote:** "So we. It take us a lot of time and then an effort to deliver one model, additional model and as convenience, no way for us to measure if it makes we make profits or not."
- **Severity:** 🔴 High
- **Impact:** Inability to make informed pricing and resource allocation decisions for custom models, potentially leading to losses.

### Pain 2: Lack of Granular Cost Tracking
- **Description:** Difficulty in allocating costs (especially delivery team costs) accurately across different products and services (setup, ongoing, custom).
- **Quote:** "But the problem that we had is for the cost we for now we can separate the cost for setup the cost for custom." "Yeah, because for example for delivery team then they serve for everything."
- **Severity:** 🟡 Medium
- **Impact:** Difficulty in understanding the true cost structure of different products and services, hindering profitability analysis.

### Pain 3: Manual Time Tracking & Lack of Categorization
- **Description:** Manual time tracking processes are insufficient for accurately allocating operational time to different products and services, especially distinguishing between setup, ongoing, and custom work.
- **Quote:** "So it's manual and they just sign the. And measure the. Measure the time they spent on one project." "But then do they know if it's set up ongoing."
- **Severity:** 🟡 Medium
- **Impact:** Inability to accurately allocate operational costs, hindering profitability analysis and capacity planning.

### Pain 4: Data Integrity Issues
- **Description:** Concerns about the reliability of existing data, including retention rates and general data cleanliness, making it difficult to trust financial reports.
- **Quote:** "The other I know I saw in 90 that you flagged the retention rate of the 94% is really like 88%." "Those are like, like, in other words, you can't rely on the data."
- **Severity:** 🔴 High
- **Impact:** Undermines the credibility of financial reports and hinders data-driven decision-making.
