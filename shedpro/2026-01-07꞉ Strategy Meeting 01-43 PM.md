# Executive Summary

This strategic advisory meeting focused on ShedPro's critical onboarding challenges and competitive positioning, with Hudson providing financial guidance on revenue optimization through faster customer activation. Aaron provided detailed operational context on current onboarding bottlenecks, including complex feature mapping, pricing configuration, and project management challenges that significantly impact time-to-value. The discussion revealed that onboarding speed represents ShedPro's single biggest competitive advantage opportunity, with current inefficiencies preventing faster revenue recognition and creating switching costs that work against customer acquisition. Key strategic decisions centered on team restructuring, process standardization, and addressing critical product gaps (particularly porch configurations and window/door features) that are causing customer churn and preventing competitive wins against Idea Room.

## Strategic Decisions

### **Team Restructuring for Specialized Onboarding**
- **Decision**: Separate Product Consultants (PCs) into specialized onboarding vs. maintenance teams
- **Rationale**: "Maintenance is empathy, care, slower understanding. Onboarding is negotiation, pushing for clarity, pressing forward, things that create action" - Hudson
- **Expected Impact**: Faster requirements gathering and execution leading to quicker MRR activation

### **Product-Specific Specialization**
- **Decision**: Structure PCs to have deep product knowledge rather than generalist approach
- **Rationale**: "The fastest PCs do is they know the product or your product well enough and deep enough to be able to look through all the features and requirements and say, okay, this is net new. This is something that we can modify existing structure with" - Aaron
- **Expected Impact**: Reduced onboarding complexity and faster technical decision-making

### **Onboarding Process Standardization**
- **Decision**: Implement clearer scope boundaries and automated data migration tools
- **Implementation**: Tools to "crawl, get everything, and then that can be your template of what we would build for you"
- **Client Management**: More aggressive approach to stalled projects with defined timelines and reactivation fees

## Pricing Strategy

### **Current Onboarding Challenges**
- **Complexity**: Price mapping "ideally should be a formula, but in practice you have people that say this specific configuration cost X... which then breaks the formula"
- **Custom Requirements**: Clients often have pricing exceptions that don't fit standard formulas
- **Scope Creep**: Additional features requested outside initial scope during onboarding

### **Competitive Benchmarking**
- **Idea Room Comparison**: "Their setup fees is like minimum of like $9,000 minimum... and they also take 6-12 weeks"
- **ShedPro Advantage**: Faster onboarding and better service quality
- **Strategic Insight**: "People might switch for price, but they stay for service" - Hudson

## Critical Product Gaps

### **Porch Configuration System** ⚠️ HIGH PRIORITY
- **Current Limitation**: Each porch style (front, side, L-shape) treated as separate building model
- **Client Request**: "All those building styles has to live in the same structure"
- **Business Impact**: "The biggest bottleneck that is preventing lots of [clients from switching] to standard building"
- **Timeline**: Goal to resolve by end of Q1

### **Window and Door Trim System** ⚠️ CRITICAL
- **Technical Challenge**: Cannot properly handle adjacent windows/doors with shared trim
- **Current Workaround**: Creating separate models for double/triple/quad windows instead of modular approach
- **Client Impact**: "One of the clients who signed up with us then cancelled because of that"
- **Status**: Window system resolved, door/transom system still in development

## Financial Metrics & Revenue Insights

### **Onboarding Impact on Revenue**
- **Key Insight**: "If we can figure out tools or something to speed up onboarding, the revenue unlock is really big" - Hudson
- **Switching Cost Factor**: "Half of it's cost, but half of it is just time... I don't want to be down $10,000 and the system isn't ready for two years"
- **Competitive Advantage**: Every week/day of onboarding reduction "fundamentally change[s] how fast they get that product to be sticky"

### **Client Segmentation Insights**
- **Small Clients**: 50-60% are "one-man shows" - owner handles delivery, sales, shop management, and project coordination
- **Larger Clients**: Have dedicated project managers but still face communication challenges
- **Service Quality**: Strong retention due to consistent delivery - "clients who started with us from day one... we just keep delivering like every feature every week"
